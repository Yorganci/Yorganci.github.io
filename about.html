§01 about

I like knowing
why it works.

I'm Bunyan, a Computer Systems Engineering student at Carleton University. I'm drawn to firmware, embedded software, operating systems and testing: the layers where a wrong assumption shows up as a stuck process or a motor that won't stop.

systems thinking

From events to behaviour

Operating-systems and real-time courses gave me practice with interrupts, process control, scheduling, shared memory and semaphores. I enjoy tracing behaviour through code and checking it against a model, including when it reveals a livelock.

hands on

Software meets I/O

I've been repairing computers since 2017, and my labs have taken me from Raspberry Pi GPIO and motor drivers to bare-metal C on MSP432 and Zynq boards. Right now I'm writing the motor control for a team's autonomous snowplow robot.
